# VOID R06 material input and output physical acceptance

This document supersedes the unsealed alphabet-coding proposal, preserved unchanged in `01_PRESERVED_INPUTS/R06_SUPERSEDED_UNSEALED_HUMAN_IO/`.

First characterize physical variable-impedance ACT_01–ACT_08 devices individually without assuming their human keycap labels are computable states. Record real resistance versus stimulus, force hysteresis, displacement, crosstalk and instrument calibration; repeat with multiple actuation elements physically stimulated at the same time. Record both real FIELD_A/B and CORE PROBE_A/B, CHARGE_A/B waveforms from the SAME supply and material core. The two AXIS wipers require real position versus voltage recording. BOUNDARY_A/B require actual external electrode-voltage/current observation.

For real visual output, use actual 3.3V measured light rail and physical LEDs or an actual powered high-Z X-Y monitor. Record both LED optical behavior and collector current and verify it varies with actual charge-state potentials. Actual optical measurements are separate from nominal component calculations.

The materials' useful state count, any alleged nonlinear memory, hysteresis, or decoding capacity must be demonstrated by repeatable physical state preparation/retention/readout at more than one level, with error and energy recorded; no bit/qubit label is assigned to unverified capacitor voltage levels. No user-readable text or general-purpose display is claimed from two LEDs.

Use existing `VOID_RAW_PHYSICAL_EVIDENCE_REGISTER.tsv` and blank `VOID_GATE_K_PHYSICAL_TRANSIENTS.tsv`, along with `VOID_R06_MULTIMODAL_PHYSICAL_MEASUREMENT_FIELDS.tsv`. Their measurement rows remain empty until physically acquired data is present. Keep K01–K04 source-on/off/Johnson/terminated controls and physical coupling comparisons separate.
