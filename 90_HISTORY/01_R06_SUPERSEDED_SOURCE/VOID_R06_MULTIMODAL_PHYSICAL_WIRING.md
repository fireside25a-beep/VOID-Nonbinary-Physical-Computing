# VOID R06 integrated physical keyboard, text-entry field and mechanical pointer

## One circuit, not a parallel execution fork

Only `02_PHYSICAL_CIRCUITS/VOID_K06_INTEGRATED_PHYSICAL_CONTINUUM.cir` is the active integrated design. It references **one** inherited `VOID_K_MESH_V1.lib`, **one** `VOID_K05_WEIGHTED_NETWORK.lib`, and **one** `VOID_K06_MECHANICAL_FIELD.lib`. Older K01–K05 are preserved physical controls; DACB remains historical source. No numerical noise generator, binary-bit processor, software neural-net, text decoder, MMIO driver or fake quantum circuit is used.

## Actual electrical interconnect

Isolated 24V current-limited supply -> physical breakdown junction -> capacitance -> existing input mesh -> PROBE_A and PROBE_B -> RLC and coupled CHARGE_A/CHARGE_B. Five preexisting K05 independent analogue voltage rails -> preexisting 12 resistors -> y0/y1 -> their original two AC-coupled mesh ports. Additional isolated 1.8V analogue rail -> 44 distinct material force-sensing keycaps, with 2 passive weighted branches per pad -> KEY_FIELD_A/KEY_FIELD_B -> two AC-coupled mesh ports. Same 1.8V rail -> two physically moveable 10k analogue potentiometers -> POINTER_X/POINTER_Y -> two AC-coupled mesh ports. **All four physical input paths converge on the same PROBE_A/B mesh** through capacitors, and four additional high-value resistors carry sustained key-force and pointer-position fields into the existing CHARGE_A/CHARGE_B nodes. This keeps short movements and held inputs both causally connected to the existing mesh without introducing another processing core.

The proposed board has 26 alphabet pressure caps, 10 number caps, SPACE/ENTER/BACKSPACE/PERIOD/COMMA/SHIFT/TAB plus one CLICK pressure pad. These are *human printed labels* that identify mechanical locations; no ASCII, scan codes, binary representation, or discrete symbol decoder exists in the computation path. The contact force is continuously variable. Multiple caps can be pressed at the same time, producing a combined analogue field. Distinguishing arbitrary combinations and recovering literal text is NOT an established property; interpreting characters or displaying decoded text remains an additional physical or boundary I/O challenge. Key labels and pad positions do not themselves prove language understanding.

The pointer is a **mechanically driven two-axis variable-resistance pointing fixture** (lever, geared ball or potentiometric mechanism), NOT an unmodified USB optical mouse. R_MOUSE_*_UP/DN represent two nominally 10k physical tracks and their movable wipers, centered at 5k/5k for a static netlist. The time-varying track resistance belongs to the material/mechanism; no synthetic motion trajectories are included.

## Actual materials and layout requirements

Use the manufacturer-selected FSR material with measured unpressed/pressed force curves and mechanical keycap linkage. The nominal 20 megohm `R_FSR_*` values are *released-state examples* (datasheet lists unloaded resistance >10 megohms), not programmable artificial force. On a board, replace their values with real sensor terminals and record per-pad resistance versus force. All 44 are physically independent components. Manufacturer series example: Interlink FSR 400, https://www.interlinkelectronics.com/downloads/datasheets/fsr-400-series-datasheet.pdf . Force-to-resistance is subject to hysteresis, drift and actuation-geometry changes. Externally held 1.8V rail is a reference hypothesis, not a supply automatically embedded in a PC CPU.

Place input pad/sensor wiring away from the avalanche source and sensitive high-impedance AC probes, label separate return path and current monitoring. Bring out KEY_FIELD_A/KEY_FIELD_B, POINTER_X/POINTER_Y, PROBE_A/PROBE_B, CHARGE_A/CHARGE_B, y0/y1 to separately labeled *high-impedance measurement headers*. **If 10 megohm loading is supplied by the instrument, do not solder its representative SPICE resistor as a second shunt.** Isolated supplies and chassis/oscilloscope ground require a qualified physical safety review before connection. Avoid direct powered connection from the 24V breakdown circuit to exposed key mechanisms; key-field is fed only from the separately current-limited low-voltage rail.

## Build and direct physical verification

First unpowered continuity inspect every key pad to VREF/KEY_FIELD_A/B, both potentiometers, AC-blocking capacitors, common returns, and the original K06 diode/current-limiting path. With isolated low-voltage field supply only, independently measure held/unheld key resistance and both pointer wiper voltages. Confirm simultaneous A+E pressing produces two continuous physical port changes, and force/depth changes are not merely discrete on/off. Confirm physical motion moves POINTER_X and POINTER_Y and only then test the AC-connected mesh with independently acquired traces. On the unchanged preexisting circuit, measure input/output power, settle under multiple source conditions, and preserve original raw measurement evidence. No unmeasured physical reading is inserted by this release.

## Scientific scope

This is a **physically realizable circuit specification**, not a fabricated or powered physical experiment. The charge capacitors are volatile analogue states; no stable multilevel RBit, no physical neural learning and no demonstrated input-text recognition is claimed. Additional sensors and output actuators can connect to existing analogue ports only after an actual task demonstrates value; audio is optional, not the definition of the machine. Koopman/Finsler/Holonomy remain separate research hypotheses, not automatic properties of this circuit.
