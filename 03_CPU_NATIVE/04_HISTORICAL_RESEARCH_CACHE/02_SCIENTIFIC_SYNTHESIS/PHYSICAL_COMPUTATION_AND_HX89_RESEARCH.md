# Research: physical computing, HX89 cycles and existing machines

**Evidence level:** research synthesis built from individually identified 1990–2026 published sources, plus sealed R08 readback. **No new hardware experiment, device control, waveform data, CPU internal connection or HX89 package execution in this research turn.**

## 1. Research scope; stop the software-to-material semantic substitution

The desired VOID operation is *causal material transformation*. Electrical current, charge, magnetic flux, heat, stochastic transport, structural deformation or optical phase must carry the state forward by their actual dynamics. A symbolic reversible rule can propose a pattern of physical interactions, but renaming a CPU word as a physical state does not change the substrate executing it. A physical implementation is indicated by an actual input port, material degree of freedom, coupling, measured response and output dependence on that material response.

The user explicitly prefers repurposing existing x86, Arm, RISC-V and other computer hardware before inventing a new board. This is the major architectural research question, not a conclusion about present-day accessible silicon. Existing processors embody continuous physical processes even though their familiar interfaces expose digital conventions. The investigator's job is to find and experimentally qualify **access paths to useful physical dynamics**, not assume they are absent or that software can arbitrarily address every transistor junction. A direct analog operation, side-channel observation, processor-managed entropy source, bus-connected mixed signal device and simulation represent different evidence classes.

**Important comparison**: a reported HX89 full cycle may offer an exact reversible transformation discipline, state scheduling, local graph movement and integrity checking. These properties are valuable even if its present execution remains software-based. The needed connection to VOID would be demonstrated when a real physical subsystem transforms a prepared boundary state, and its measured output causally changes following the HX89-directed operation. That connection cannot be established from names, random seeds or a checksum alone.

## 2. Primary literature lines and their actionable implications

### 1990s: silicon as dynamics; reversible systems; nonlinear flows

Mead (1990, S001) motivates treating elementary physical effects in silicon and **relative** analog signals as the quantities doing useful work. Roska and Chua (1993, S003; 1993 development report S004) describe local coupled analog arrays; notably, a digital development environment around the substrate does not automatically mean that the array's physical evolution was simulated. Their established models are not VOID's implemented hardware.

Fredkin (1990, S002) and related reversible cellular automaton theory offer locality, exact reversible transformations and finite-state dynamics. These are mathematical constructs and sometimes engineered digital cellular machines. They are the correct adversarial comparison for the HX89 full cycle, but not sufficient evidence for thermodynamic or analog execution. Strogatz (1994, S005) gives geometric methods for oscillation and bifurcation; Margolus and Levitin (1998, S006) examine physically distinguishable transitions, rather than equating an arbitrarily large coordinate count with available physical capacity.

### 2000s: material state, physical disorder and collective state models

Lloyd (2000, S007) and Ott (2002 edition, S008) provide physical-state and nonlinear dynamics perspectives. Pappu et al. (2002, S009) demonstrate a real disordered optical medium producing an identifying response; this shows a material is causal in producing a response, but the authentication task itself is not general-purpose computation.

Adamatzky et al. (2005, S010) cover reaction-diffusion wave substrates, which are genuinely material transformations even if different from CPU silicon. Port-Hamiltonian formalism (S011) offers disciplined accounting for energy storage, dissipation and interconnection in physical networks. Strukov et al. (2008, S012) show nanoscale coupled ionic and electronic transport gives stateful I-V behavior; the work suggests how to distinguish lasting material memory from plain capacitor discharge.

Kanerva (2009, S013) develops *mathematical* high-dimensional distributed representations, usually executed as arrays of symbols. This provides a useful comparator but must not be imported into VOID as proof of physical dimensions. Kish (2009, S014) is worth comparing because its signals are real stochastic carriers; the interpretation as logic states can be kept separate from the underlying physical processes. Rowley et al. (2009, S015) and Schmid (2010, S016) permit external analysis of experimentally observed modes; Koopman and DMD processing are analysis tools and not a substitute for material execution.

### 2010s: experimental dynamic processing, coupled oscillation and material memory

Appeltant et al. (2011, S017) demonstrate information-processing dynamics with nonlinear delayed feedback. The experiment is especially relevant when asking whether an HX89 'cycle' can be expressed as a repeated, causally linked *physical trajectory*; virtual nodes in the conventional reservoir are not automatically independently physical nodes. Prezioso et al. (2015, S019) show a measured memristive crossbar carrying out classifier-relevant transformations, providing an instructive distinction between physical and external learning/readout functions.

Piccinini (2015, S020) gives a mechanistic challenge: not every physical process or correspondence between numbers and measured quantities should be called computation. The question is what the machine *causally does under interventions*. Inagaki et al. (2016, S021) supply an optical example of coupled-oscillator optimization. Tanaka et al. (2019, S022) summarize material reservoir realizations and architecture-dependent use of analog and digital stages.

### 2020s: native material adaptation and physical energetics

Liang et al. (2024, S023) review electronic reservoir topologies and fully analog/mixed-signal implementations. Shougat et al. (2024, S024) demonstrate an adaptive oscillator with physical plasticity, relevant to persistent / tunable material state rather than software-maintained coefficients. Manzano et al. (2024, S025), the 2024 stochastic thermodynamics discussion (S026), and Helms et al. (2025, S027) provide tools to reason about trajectory-dependent dissipation, retention and response. These are not proofs of a VOID substrate; their role in the cache is diagnostics and design comparison. Recent 2026 studies (S028–S031) add experimental routes using oxide transistor memories, optically controlled memristive reservoirs, integrated silicon photonics and magnonic-optical delay structures. Their specific physical media differ, but the common lesson is that processing claims require the material path itself to transform an input and generate an observed output. They do not imply that the same features are software-addressable inside every commodity CPU.

## 3. What HX89 could contribute, to be checked against actual supplied source

**User-reported HX89 historical description, not yet source-verified in this cache:** five-dimensional hexworld, Q²-related structures, a reversible cyclic law of the form Θχ Fχ^t Θχ Fχ^t = identity, a finite torus certificate, chiral and spatial movements, and native executable experiments. This is *not* proof that HX89 is an analog VM or that it accesses transistor physics outside ordinary instruction execution.

The scientific value of a full cycle may be found in these distinct layers, to be matched in the source:

1. **Reversible state rule**: actual inverse semantics, exact state root, conserved quantities and errors that appear under repeated cycles.
2. **Locality / topology**: which interactions are neighbors and which are transported, and whether a five-dimensional index is a coordinate schema or independent physical degrees of freedom.
3. **Causal physical mapping**: which voltages, currents, oscillatory phases, mixed-signal features or directly measurable machine phenomena are moved or modulated when the cycle executes.
4. **Measurement / feedback**: whether physical outcomes determine the next change, or whether a predetermined software trace simply runs and is relabeled.
5. **Cross-machine invariance**: what survives across x86, Arm, RISC-V or other machines because it is a demonstrated hardware operation, rather than a portable numerical routine.

A *potential* conversion would be: user action -> genuine physical preparation -> HX89-informed causal interaction cycle -> independently observed material evolution -> physical output with counterfactual dependence. This is a **research candidate causal chain**, not code or an implemented diagram.

## 4. Methods for detecting a new physical primitive without falling into binary conventions

Do not begin by choosing how many 'bits' a node represents. Begin with the material state manifold and its observable transitions. At least four independent tests describe a candidate primitive:

- **Distinct physical state**: for deliberately varied input histories, is there an experimentally distinguishable continuous or multistable state of the substrate, with a recorded location, unit and calibration method?
- **Composability**: can multiple distinct physical nodes influence one another through traceable interactions, rather than a digital controller calculating their responses?
- **Persistence or cycle integrity**: after perturbation, what remains of charge, phase, magnetic flux, conductance or topology and which transitions occur naturally?
- **Task causality**: can a physical response be used to produce a distinguishable task-relevant output, and does removing a specific material coupling change that outcome while other channels are controlled?

Do not turn these tests into letters or Roman numerals. X/V/I are meaningful as *physical quantities* only where they identify a measured flux, potential, current, exchange path or material mode. A name does not define a new universal state system.

## 5. Mathematics is diagnostic, not the computing substrate

The following are **analysis contracts only** to be applied to physical measurements, not waveform generators or code running in the material path. Their purpose is to identify independent experimental quantities and distinguish alternative mechanisms.

**Charge/current causality**: dq_i/dt = sum_j I_{ji} + I_ext,i. With q_i = C_i V_i for a linear capacitor, inspect actual current/charge pathways. In nonlinear devices, characterize q_i(V_i,history) from measured response rather than using a constant C blindly.

**Energy flow and interconnection**: H(x) can account for 1/2 C V², 1/2 L I² and additional nonlinear energy. Port-Hamiltonian form x_dot = [J(x) - R(x)] grad H(x) + G u, where J is antisymmetric and R is positive-semidefinite, distinguishes conservative exchange from loss. Requires suitable physically characterized state and constitutive equations. Supported method: S011.

**Resistance/coupling**: I_ij = G_ij(x,t) [V_i - V_j] if this constitutive relationship is applicable. Measure deviations and hysteresis; don't assume every internal path is an ohmic resistor. S012, S019.

**Noise spectral density**: one-sided classical Johnson voltage spectrum S_V ≈ 4 k_B T R [V²/Hz] in the appropriate frequency/temperature regime. This predicts statistical scale; it does **not** predict an individual future fluctuation trace. Compare real calibrated readings, bandwidth and instrument noise.

**Cycle and physical non-reversal**: A map T can be mathematically invertible, T^-1(T(x)) = x. A real dissipative physical state x(t) need not reverse when you apply a matching discrete inverse instruction. Measure residual state, energy/entropy flow and path dependence rather than inferring exact thermodynamic reversibility from algebra. S002, S025, S027.

**Observable geometry**: real measured y(t)=h(x(t)) can be analyzed for mode structure, control observability, phase lag, synchronization and physical state rank. Koopman/DMD are offline diagnostics only, S015–S016. A numerical high-dimensional embedding does not create physical modes, S013.

**Stochastic physical evolution**: diffusion/noise process models can be used to analyze distributions and compare hypotheses. Do not synthesize a replacement noise process and call it measured CPU or K06 noise.
