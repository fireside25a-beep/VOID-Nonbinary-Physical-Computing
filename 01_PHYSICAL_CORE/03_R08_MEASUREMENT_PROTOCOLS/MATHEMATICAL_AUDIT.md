# Corrections that remain mandatory

Koopman: K_t g(x)=g(Phi_t(x)) for deterministic flow; its linearity in functions does not implement infinite-dimensional evolution on a finite PCB. For stochastic dynamics use a Markov expectation operator subject to assumptions. No arbitrary sin/cos physical circuit is a claimed Koopman processor. The term dPsi/dt=L Psi+Sigma_thermal does not follow generally from stochastic Koopman theory without specifying stochastic calculus and observation process. A finite DMD model's held-out prediction performance must be measured.

Finsler: a non-quadratic directional norm is a mathematical metric, not a built physical nonreciprocal device. Linear passive reciprocal networks under appropriate conditions have reciprocal S parameters; any directional asymmetry must be measured with power-normalized ports, identical terminations, and thermal/nonlinear controls. This gate is not currently active.

Holonomy: theta_G = contour integral A·dS requires a well-defined connection and appropriate physical transport; an arbitrary RLC phase lag does not prove Berry phase or topological memory. There is no unconditional immunity to thermal noise, hackers, crashes or energy loss. Physical residual phase must be separated from dynamic delay and calibration drift in its own later gate.

p-adic: non-Archimedean valuation is a mathematical construction, not guaranteed realizable as a physical electrical ultrametric or state-corruption shield. It is not mixed into the current real-valued electrical laws without a consistent interface and experimental necessity.

Physical thermodynamics: passive Johnson noise does not imply freely available computational work. Powered reverse breakdown uses external energy. No thermodynamic performance claim without including sensor/receiver/bias/analysis instrumentation energy. These circuit specifications make no physical entropy certification or cryptographic assurance claim.
