# VOID R06 physical material-state research readback

The proposed `I`, `V`, `X` / "RBit" vocabulary is an **unproven candidate for material states**, not a Roman number encoding, software symbol table, digital bit, or simulated qubit. The actual K06 primitive operations are physical charge, current, flux, nonlinear conduction, force-driven conductance, mechanical position, light emission and electrical coupling.

Three capacitively retained node potentials `CHARGE_A/B/C` form a simultaneous continuous physical state vector, each with physical capacitive retention, resistance/leakage and charge-to-charge feedback. An existing inductive branch introduces flux and reactive energy storage. The anti-parallel diode cross-bridge provides a physically nonlinear path. These can serve as candidate substrates for multilevel response, but three capacitors do not by themselves implement three stable levels or an exponentially larger state space.

**Actual next physical demonstration** is to construct the relevant component network and apply repeatable material stimuli while measuring simultaneous multichannel voltages/currents and readout power. Demonstrate at least three stable *distinguishable* physical response regions, transitions, state retention/hysteresis and repeatability, compared with ordinary analog charge relaxation; only then qualify a new multilevel memory cell. Simultaneous coupled interactions are distinct from time-scanned digital updates, but their energy and latency must be measured. Distinguish classical analog, classical noisy, and genuinely quantum evidence without renaming any category.

The inherited historical NeuroMantle QEC/SNN software and DACB RDRAND/ISA entropy archives are preserved, not injected into the machine's material core. The real CPU physical entropy blocks are a possible future physically identified substrate; raw internal noise nodes must first be located by measured hardware access before any corresponding on-die K06 topology is asserted. No digital random-number instruction is a replacement for the proposed physical source-to-mesh connection.
