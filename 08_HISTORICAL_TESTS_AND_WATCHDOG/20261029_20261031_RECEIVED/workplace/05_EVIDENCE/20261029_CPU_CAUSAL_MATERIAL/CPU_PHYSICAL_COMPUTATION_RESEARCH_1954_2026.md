# VOID/HX89 physical CPU research and full-machine material-causality ledger

2026-10-29. This document is an engineering hypothesis/evidence review, not a claim of novel silicon functionality. The original M00–M115 rows, HX89 engines, R08 K06 sources, archival checksums and source histories are authoritative and are retained unchanged.

## Criterion for a useful material VOID operator

A genuinely new physically nonbinary computing operation requires all the following together: (1) a controllable physical stimulus; (2) a known substrate with a reproducible continuous or other nonbinary state; (3) independently obtainable physical observables, not a host calculation of the expected result; (4) a measured multistate, nonlinear or associative transformation causally dependent on substrate evolution; (5) feedback after physical evolution; (6) retention, separability, read/write endurance and disturbance characterization; (7) rejection of confounders including conventional binary cache state, CPU scheduling, stale measurements, fixed software state and mathematical simulator output; (8) end-to-end usefulness under multi-node/collective loads. A physically implemented binary processor executing code does not by itself meet the new operator criterion.

## Historical source crosswalk, useful and limiting observations

- **1954 — Semiconductor negative resistance and parametric oscillators.** Shockley investigated transistor/diode negative resistance; Eiichi Goto invented LC-parametron logic using resonant parametric excitation. These are physical oscillators, with usable coupling and phase. Most historical parametrons deliberately settled into two stable phase states and implemented binary logic. Research value for VOID: simultaneous coupled resonance and alternative multi-phase trajectories, not a pre-existing nonbinary commodity CPU function. Sources: https://www.nokia.com/bell-labs/publications-and-media/publications/negative-resistance-arising-from-transit-time-in-semiconductor-diodes/ ; https://museum.ipsj.or.jp/en/heritage/parametron.html ; https://museum.ipsj.or.jp/en/computer/dawn/0016.html .
- **1959–1960 — Silicon MOSFET physical field control.** Atalla and Kahng demonstrated MOS field-effect amplification. A MOSFET is a semiconductor/electrostatic physical device with continuous terminal behavior; the ordinary x86 ISA is an interface to engineered digital functions, not to arbitrary MOS gate pads. Source: https://www.computerhistory.org/siliconengine/metal-oxide-semiconductor-mos-transistor-demonstrated/ .
- **1961 — Landauer thermodynamic accounting.** Irreversible logic and heat are physically linked. This does not imply that heat from a digital calculation automatically becomes useful material computation. Source: https://doi.org/10.1147/RD.53.0183 .
- **1989–1991 — Mead/Andreou/Boahen and current-mode subthreshold CMOS.** Analog VLSI and associative memory demonstrate actual use of exponential weak-inversion silicon transfer and collective currents. Physical implementation requires appropriate biased transistor geometries, connections and measurement; cannot assume ordinary CPU internal gates can be arbitrarily biased with a user instruction. Sources: https://carvermead.caltech.edu/research ; https://web.stanford.edu/group/brainsinsilicon/documents/91_journ_IEEEtnn_Current.pdf .
- **2008 — Resistive memory/ionic transport.** Strukov and colleagues measured stateful nanoscale electric/ionic devices with hysteretic conductance. This suggests genuine material write/read memory and collective current operations, but is not an existing x86/ARM CPU instruction. Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature06932 .
- **2015 — Cross-core thermal remanence.** Masti et al. experimentally showed CPU core heat outlasts instructions and couples between cores, transmitting up to 12.5 bps on a particular platform. Relevant to VOID physical temporal memory, but temperature channels and performance depend on the specific package, thermal resistance and observation methods. Source: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity15/technical-sessions/presentation/masti .
- **2022–2025 — Power-dependent timing/frequency.** Hertzbleed demonstrated power-data-dependent dynamic frequency scaling affecting CPU timing. Newer work reported power-to-timing behavior even without a simple frequency-based channel. These make real processor power response a plausible observation path; they are not direct independent analog voltage measurements, and KVM can mask the real hardware. Sources: https://www.usenix.org/conference/usenixsecurity22/presentation/wang-yingchen ; https://www.hertzbleed.com/scheduled-disclosure-sp2025.pdf .
- **2024 — Physical reservoir and plastic oscillator computing.** Physical reservoir reviews and oscillator prototypes show mechanical, electrical, photonic or material dynamics can implement useful multi-dimensional temporal transformations. Dedicated oscillators are distinct from our host's CPU silicon unless a real coupling is demonstrated. Sources: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41928-024-01133-z ; https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.109.064205 .
- **2025–2026 — Memory-array current arithmetic.** Published SRAM-CIM and measured RRAM-CIM chips explicitly exploit analog bitline current and physical multilevel conductance, potentially closer to collective VOID arithmetic than a single thermal bit. These are specialized memory macros, not accessible as an arbitrary commodity CPU cache instruction. Sources: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11192607 ; https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11471816 .

## Next decisive physical-causality discriminators, not completed

1. **Thermal remanence:** randomized, equal-duration activity and idle groups on the same core, **identical readout workload afterward**, staggered recovery windows, measure temperature/power when calibrated sensors exist, compare to cache-only and scheduling controls; seek a reversible continuous relaxation trend rather than digital occupancy alone.
2. **Power-frequency coupling:** compare constant-bit-count different switching activity at fixed work, capture effective frequency and wall time, hold temperature and scheduling as covariates, compare bare-metal versus KVM; do not call a timing difference an analog voltage reading.
3. **Subthreshold weak-inversion array:** demonstrate real gate-bias control and measured continuous drain current at several calibrated levels, then simultaneously couple many transistors and test nonlinear spatial operations, disturbance, temperature coefficients, and independent held-out input–output responses.
4. **Collective AC/DC K06:** real safe physical current injection/readout with independently calibrated instruments, simultaneous perturbations at multiple charge nodes; measure nonlinear interaction, phase, energy, write/hold/read, retention and cross-coupling before labelling memory or polyform operations physical.
5. **Counterfactual proof:** remove/shuffle the physical response; verify HX89's next decision loses the corresponding influence, while the digital source program and stimulus remain identical. Exclude all feedback based only on host-generated predicted voltages or a numerical emulator.

Keep working toward a larger, batch/coupled native material machine, not an isolated SHA-256, spike, side-channel or re-encoded binary toy. The exact physical architecture remains to be established by real causal proof.
