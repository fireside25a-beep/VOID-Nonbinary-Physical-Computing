# Mandatory execution rules

1. Original archives and historical milestone statuses are immutable.
2. One canonical cumulative planner and evidence ledger, M00–M115 exactly once.
3. Every PASS must have a real executed command, bounded runtime and unedited output.
4. Every FAIL must remain in the append-only watchdog journal; fixing code requires rerun.
5. No missing hardware channel may be replaced by simulated measurement or a mock sample.
6. Do not equate x86 instructions, software hexworld or TSC timings with proven raw charge-state computation.
7. Do not assert fundamental impossibility of new uses of silicon; test proposed access and causal coupling.
8. Differentiate native concurrent SIMD execution from simultaneous independent analog material states.
9. Physical computations require experimentally demonstrated causal inputs, state, output and independent controls.
10. Keep development tooling separate from the experimental VOID substrate.
11. Avoid any undocumented arbitrary MMIO access, physical electrical overdrive, or destructive disk/source changes.
12. The final goal remains one batch-capable, collective, useful physical nonbinary machine with persistence, feedback, I/O and recovery.
13. Experiments can fail and must be recorded accurately. Verification cannot be waived, substituted, or fabricated.
