# VOID R08 K06 — source-linked low-cost assembly and verification

## What this document is

This guide is derived from `01_PHYSICAL_CORE/01_R08_ACTIVE_SPICE/VOID_K06_INTEGRATED_PHYSICAL_CONTINUUM.cir` and its **three actually included** `.lib` modules. The corresponding exact card expansion is `K06_HIERARCHICAL_EXPANDED_BOM.csv` (175 primitive devices and 74 nets). `K06_PROTOTYPE_CONNECTOR_PINOUT.csv` enumerates 15 proposed connector positions/groups comprising 78 explicitly numbered pins. This is an experimentally buildable **electrical assembly contract**, not an issued PCB fabrication package. No mechanical footprint or supplier pinout has been assigned without checking a purchased part.

Each connector pin is a proposed **physical interface**, not a SPICE component. The two copies of the same net on different connectors are intended connections and do not create a new independent voltage source. The `0` net is circuit reference; verify any connection to a USB host or grounded oscilloscope before plugging it in.

## Five construction modules (one core and four supports)

**CORE — K06 R08.** Populate the actual charge network (`R_INTEG_A/B/C`, `C_CHARGE_A/B/C`, leakage, coupling and feedback), K01 junction branch, K05 resistor network, mesh, nonlinear anti-parallel pair, protected WRITE/BOUNDARY connections, and optional visual/piezo loads according to the source. A symbol representing a SPICE supply `V_*` is a **terminal supplied by a real instrument**, not a device to solder.

**P1 — separated power.** `PWR_01`: pins 1/2 `SUPPLY/0` (nominal current-limited 24 V only for K01), pins 3/4 `UI_SRC/0` (nominal 1.8 V), pins 5/6 `DISPLAY_3V3/0` (nominal 3.3 V). Preserve the in-source `R_UI_CURRENT=100Ω` between `UI_SRC` and `UI_1V8`. `R_CURRENT_LIMIT=3.3kΩ` on the 24 V branch should have at least 0.5 W rating pending real temperature/current checks; at a hypothetical short-to-return, 24²/3300 ≈0.175 W. Fit a removable power disconnect, current-limit setting, and rail test point. No unqualified fixed-24-V regulator is endorsed.

**P2 — observation.** `CORE_TEST_01` carries `SHUNT_TOP`, `JCT`, `PROBE_A/B`, `CHARGE_A/B/C` interleaved with return. `OBS_01` carries `FIELD_A/B`, `AXIS_A/B`, `DISPLAY_X/Y/Z` similarly. Original mesh sources model 1 MΩ + 30 pF probe loading; other nodes use explicit 10 MΩ instrument-equivalent resistors. Choose **one actual load** consistent with the reference circuit (soldered load or instrument equivalent), never duplicate it inadvertently. A properly powered FET-input buffer with verified common-mode/headroom and calibrated loading is preferable for the higher-impedance work; a 1 MΩ oscilloscope probe is not an interchangeable electrometer. Source-model-vs-instrument impedance belongs in the experimental record.

**P3 — actual acquisition and stimulus.** `IO_STIM_01`: WRITE_A/0, WRITE_B/0, BOUNDARY_A/0, BOUNDARY_B/0. The K06 source already includes 1 MΩ write bleeds, 10 nF series coupling, 100 kΩ charge injection resistors, diode clamps to `UI_1V8`/return and bounded boundary branches. These do not excuse omitting protection, host isolation, buffer headroom checks, current limiting, a hardware output-disable, calibration, and a verified ADC/DAC connector. Before any driver, use real manually controlled applied voltages and record their true values. Never apply 24 V to these low-voltage stimulus connectors. Do not represent a logic input toggle or an offline CSV as a physical DAC command.

**P4 — material/contact carrier.** `MAT_11`, `MAT_12`, `MAT_13`, `MAT_14`, `MAT_21`, `MAT_22`, `MAT_23`, `MAT_24` each expose `UI_1V8` and one unique `X_MATERIAL/PAD_nn` net, as in the expanded K06 hierarchy. Mount the actual contact or removable, measured nominal 10 MΩ element **between those two pins**. On the receiving sheet, each `PAD_nn` then passes through its **separate fixed 1 kΩ series resistor `R_ACT_GUARD_nn`** to `X_MATERIAL/F_nn`. Those eight resistors are not eight extra replaceable sensors and are *not* a 1 kΩ grounded perimeter guard. Keep the actual 22 kΩ sheet links and 220 kΩ returns exactly as specified. `AXIS_POT_A/B` have `UI_1V8`, `0`, and wiper `AXIS_A/B`; physical 10 kΩ potentiometer tracks replace the nominal pair of 5 kΩ half-track equations in the source. Record actual wiper voltages. Preserve exact 2×4 spatial order in a labeled, low-leakage, removable carrier.

`K05_BIAS_01` exposes exact `xp1/xm1/xp2/xm2/xp3` and `y0/y1` with returns. K05's original source uses independent nominal bias values including **negative** source voltages. Do not directly attach these nodes to a unipolar 1.8 V or 3.3 V DAC without a separately validated bipolar/level-shifting network.

## Build and measurement sequence

1. **No power:** check every resistor/capacitor marking, diode cathode and transistor C/B/E pinout against the chosen manufacturer; map each fitted device to its `instance_path`, source file/line and two or three nets. Independently confirm continuity and no unintended rail shorts. Document input connector orientation and any substitutions.
2. **Passive low-voltage network:** assemble the mesh, weighted K05 network, three charge capacitors and reciprocal coupling without K01 24 V, displays or USB-host wiring. Excite from measured low-voltage bench sources, capture charge/PROBE trajectories and repeat with power disconnected and state fully discharged.
3. **Nonlinear/material intervention:** fit the two 1N4148 nonlinear junction diodes, eight material contacts and two analogue axis tracks. Measure release-state resistance of every contact and vary **one** physical contact at a time. Reject runs with unrecorded replacement parts or unmeasured probe loading.
4. **Protected electrodes:** verify WRITE_A/B and BOUNDARY_A/B rail protection using a current-limited adjustable source at safe measured voltages, after checking clamp polarity and ground references. Record readback voltage at the actual electrode, not only the host command. Compare WRITE_A driven, WRITE_B driven, both disconnected, and polarity-reversed controls.
5. **K01 and outputs:** power the 24 V branch only after verifying the 3.3 kΩ current limit and device ratings. Capture JCT and input AC coupling with verified loading, then add 3.3 V display circuitry and optional ceramic output one branch at a time. A nominal piezo `15 nF` SPICE load does not prove a particular transducer or audible output. Independently record total current and source isolation.
6. **Acquisition and hardware feedback:** first collect authentic serial/time/calibration-tagged measurements and ingest them with `native_bench_ingestion/void_bench_ingest`. A future automated loop must additionally prove the named ADC/DAC driver actually read the instrument, applied a protected output and measured the resulting electrode voltage in hardware. Use an external watchdog/output inhibit and repeat A/B physical ablations to separate transport success from causal material response.

## Exact two-path acceptance

**Software baseline:** `python3 00_CONTROL/run_canonical_unified_acceptance.py` rebuilds the native solver/control and tests 6 modes, 7 scenarios each. Recorded digital voltages come from a software RC model and not a physical board.

**Physical evidence:** save a true instrument's original log, serial and calibration identifiers, as-built resistor/diode/pinout notes, ambient conditions, probe/instrument input impedance, actual supply rails and safety/current limits. The native C file-ingestion tool validates structure and SHA-256 but does **not** attest instrument identity by itself. No real physical acquisition may be replaced by simulator output merely to meet a file requirement.

**Analytical comparison:** classify actual device tolerances, leakage, instrument loading, temperature, settling times and contact states; compare synchronized real timestamps and voltages against the mathematical model with independent confidence intervals. For strong evidence, include repeated measurements, deliberate disconnected input controls, a second meter, and operator-signed provenance.

## Mechanical fabrication / release progression

Initial test carrier may use clean perfboard and short guarded/shielded measurement connections. A fabrication-ready multi-board PCB release additionally requires verified manufacturer part numbers, orientation, footprint dimensions, footprint-to-pin/electrical-symbol mapping, supply/current protection calculations, KiCad ERC, routed PCB DRC, leakage/guarding analysis, artwork, drill files, assembly drawing and independently checked Gerbers. None is inferred from schematic source text or sample electrical data.
